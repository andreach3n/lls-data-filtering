MNIST Piece Viewer

How to read this
Every weight matrix of this 784→128→10 MLP is a sum of rank-one pieces (128 in layer 1, 32 in layer 2). A small network assigns each piece a gate per image; pieces with gate > 0.1 are important for that image. Training asks that the important pieces alone reproduce the prediction (sufficiency) and that, with them switched off, no mix of the other pieces gives the predicted digit more than chance (necessity).
Layer-1 piece: its template is a 28×28 pixel pattern; the piece's activation is the image (normalised) dotted with it. "Image × template" shows where that sum comes from, pixel by pixel. Note that the blank background is not zero after normalisation, so it contributes too.
Layer-2 piece: reads a direction in the 128 hidden units and writes to the 10 digit logits ("writes" bars). Its template is that read direction pulled back to pixels through layer 1, ignoring the ReLU, so treat it as a rough picture.
Drop: change in the predicted digit's logit when only that piece is switched off in the full model. A large drop means the image leans on that piece alone; a small one means other pieces cover for it.
