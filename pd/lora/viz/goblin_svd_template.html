Goblin SVD Pieces

The SVD pieces behind figure 25g's learned sets. Each module's LoRA weight change (7 modules × 28 layers of Qwen3-1.7B-Base, fine-tuned on Dolci answers where 5% weave in goblins) is split by SVD into up to 32 rank-1 pieces, 6,272 in all. A piece reads one number per token, a = x·v (its input direction, scaled by the singular value), and adds a times its output direction u. Pick a piece to see every token where |a| is in its top 1%, on held-out prompts, each with its goblin answer and its neutral answer.

Active: pieces have no gates, so a piece counts as active at a token when |a| is in that piece's top 1% of answer tokens. Every piece is active on about 1% of answer tokens by construction; what differs is where. The shade is |a| divided by the piece's largest |a|. Columns are the share of tokens of that kind where the piece is active. Before 1st goblin = the token just before an answer's first goblin word, where the model decides to go goblin. Goblin ans, pre 1st = goblin-answer tokens before that. Goblin ans, post 1st = goblin-answer tokens after the first goblin word, except the goblin words' own tokens. Neutral ans = neutral answers, Prompt = prompt tokens.

Learned sets: green names are in the goblin set, blue names in the normal set (the sets at λ 0 of goblin_protect.py --opt 1, figure 25g). Joined at λ: the largest λ at which the piece is in the set. λ steps down from 3e-3, so 3e-3 means it was among the first pieces the set needed. Goblin lost when removed: start from the goblin set alone (everything else off), take out only this piece, and measure how much goblin left drops, in % of the fine-tune's gain in log P at goblin words, on 60 of these goblin answers. Normal lost when removed: the same for the normal set and normal kept, on 60 neutral answers.

What it writes (o and down pieces only, which add straight to the residual stream): u through the final norm and the unembedding, the tokens it pushes up and down. A piece adds a·u, so when a is negative the two lists swap. The detail panel shows the mean a at tokens just before goblin words, so you can tell which end is used there.
