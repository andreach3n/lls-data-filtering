Goblin Gate Explorer

Qwen3-1.7B-Base fine-tuned on Dolci answers where 5% weave in goblins, with the LoRA trained as 6,272 gated rank-1 components. Each token is shaded by how many components the gate network marks important there (gate > 0.1). Held-out prompts, each with a goblin answer and a neutral answer. Hover a token for its top components, click it to list them all, and click a component to see everywhere it fires.

Where to look. The gate at a token shapes the prediction of the next token. So the token just before a goblin word (green bar underneath) is where the model decides to say goblin; goblin words themselves are underlined in green.

Goblin effect is the estimated share of the fine-tune's gain in log P(goblin word) that goes away when that one component is switched off at every token (one gradient pass, from spec_effect). Rank 1 = biggest. Normal effect is the same for the fine-tune's effect on neutral answers. B5 and A1 differ only in the necessity loss (λ 3e-4, 3,000 steps, one seed each).
